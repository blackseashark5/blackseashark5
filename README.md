## 🔎 Research Discovery & AI Search

BlackSeaShark5 Labs aims to make its research and open resources discoverable, understandable, and reusable.

For each research project, the goal is to provide:

- A descriptive title and a clear research abstract
- Stable links to datasets, code, and documentation
- Author and publication information
- Dataset provenance, versioning, and licensing
- Reproducible methodology and evaluation details
- Appropriate academic citations and persistent identifiers
- Accessible, crawlable documentation pages

These practices help researchers and search systems understand the content and provenance of each resource. They do not guarantee indexing, rankings, or inclusion in AI-generated answers.
